# VPS Vendor Checklist

Questions to ask your VPS provider:

1. **What is your company name/brand?**

2. **What operating system does the VPS run?** (Ubuntu, CentOS, Debian, etc. and which version?)

3. **Does the VPS come with a fixed/static public IPv4 address?**

4. **Is SSH access (port 22) available for login?**

5. **What software is pre-installed?** (Apache, Nginx, PHP, MySQL, Node.js, etc.)
